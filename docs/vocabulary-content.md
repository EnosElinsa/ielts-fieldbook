Automatic mastery uses dated evidence independently for each learning meaning. `Familiar` requires at least three qualifying successes in each of Meaning, Listening, Spelling and Usage. `Mastered` requires five in each dimension and two externally assessed production successes. Each counted success in a dimension must be at least 24 hours after the previous counted success; multiple same-day drills cannot satisfy these thresholds. Successful objective reviews, dated source records and assessed evidence qualify; production qualifies only when externally assessed. Audio playback, card browsing, pending or self-reported use, legacy counters and undated summaries do not establish mastery. Active wrong-word recovery blocks advancement. These are transparent product thresholds informed by spaced retrieval principles, not universal scientific cutoffs; FSRS controls review scheduling separately.

Automatic status is the default. A persistent manual override is marked `Manual` and can be reset with `Use automatic`, without clearing wrong words, adding evidence or changing review dates. `statusSource` preserves the choice in account data, sync and backups. Older non-`new` labels remain manual overrides; old default `new` labels use automatic status. Entry summaries conservatively combine learned meanings; untouched dictionary meanings do not depress the learning summary. Group learning-card browsing progress is separate from assessment completion and mastery, stored in the optional `wordbookProgress.learning` payload.

`scripts/enrich-vocabulary.mjs` streams a locally downloaded Kaikki.org/Wiktionary English JSONL export, or an HTTPS JSONL URL on the official `kaikki.org` host, filters a supplied term list, and atomically writes a small attribution-bearing enrichment file. It records definitions, usage examples, IPA and available synonyms/antonyms with source/license per sense. Review generated content before publishing; Wiktionary-derived content must retain its CC-BY-SA-4.0 attribution and share-alike terms. Do not commit dictionary dumps, provider credentials, or raw authenticated exports.

```text
node scripts/enrich-vocabulary.mjs --input dictionary.jsonl --terms terms.txt --output enrichments.json
node scripts/enrich-vocabulary.mjs --input dictionary.jsonl --terms guixue-snapshot.json --output enrichments.json
```
