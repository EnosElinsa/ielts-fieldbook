Risk-driven editorial review covered **60 memberships** across all six books. **50** received reviewed complete definitions, examples and collocations; **25** have a confirmed thematic context; **35** still lack evidence of the intended group sense.

Generic chapters and test parts do not identify a sense without the original sentence. Their common modern content is improved, but context remains unresolved. Fruit-core noun and animal-mating courtship are genuinely absent from the extracted dictionary and use stable editorial IDs. All extracted courtship senses are obsolete courtly-behaviour senses; the animal meaning is not substituted onto one of those IDs. Geography core reuses its broad existing noun ID. Other corrections reuse dictionary IDs. Original dictionary credits and separately attributed editorial text are preserved. Unconfirmed inherited synonyms and word families are cleared from supplements.

Bound 99 preexisting editorial tasks to explicit sense IDs, suppressing 21 mismatched old primary sense tasks. Task correction is separate from membership review. Additionally, 35 reviewed defaults (fruit, vegetable, the 21 task mismatch words and additional fragment/archaic-priority corrections) improve unbound and standalone content; unknown group intent remains unreviewed.

All candidates and unresolved memberships are enumerated in [vocabulary-context-audit.json](vocabulary-context-audit.json). This is not exhaustive human review.
